How to play

  1. Take turns drawing. The drawer reads the card aloud. The other person answers first, then the drawer answers the same card.
  2. Listen to understand. No fixing, no correcting, no debating each other's memory of events.
  3. Move up a level when you both feel ready. Eight to twelve cards per level makes a good sitting.
  4. Each player has two passes. A pass needs no explanation.
  5. Play a Response card after an answer that landed hard or mattered.
  6. If either of you feels flooded, pause for at least 20 minutes.

Play when you're both rested and not mid-conflict. The deck is for understanding each other, not settling disputes.
